Step by step

Samsungexpand_more
  1. 1Open Settings, then Apps, then Daybloom.
  2. 2Tap Battery and choose Unrestricted.
  3. 3In Settings › Battery › Background usage limits, make sure Daybloom isn't in Sleeping apps.
Xiaomi, Redmi, POCOexpand_more

Settings › Apps › Manage apps › Daybloom: set Battery saver to No restrictions and turn on Autostart.

OnePlus, Oppo, Realmeexpand_more

Settings › Battery › Daybloom: choose Don't optimize and allow background activity.

Other phonesexpand_more

Look in Settings for Battery or App battery usage and allow Daybloom to run in the background.
